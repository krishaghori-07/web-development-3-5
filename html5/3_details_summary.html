Gujrat

Gujarat: Gujarat is a vibrant state in western India, known for its rich culture, history, and beautiful landscapes. It is famous for industries, textiles, handicrafts, and delicious Gujarati cuisine.

Rajasthan

Rajasthan is a culturally rich state in northwestern India, famous for its royal palaces, forts, deserts, and colorful traditions. It attracts visitors with its history, folk music, dance, and unique architecture.

Mumbai

Mumbai is a major city on India’s west coast and the capital of Maharashtra. It is known as the financial and entertainment capital of India, with landmarks such as the Gateway of India and Marine Drive.
