1.Mumbai-the city of dreams

Mumbai is home to many famous landmarks and tourist attractions. The Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Juhu Beach, and Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus are some of the most popular places to visit. The city is also known for its delicious street food, especially vada pav, pav bhaji, and bhel puri. Mumbai has a strong economy based on finance, entertainment, business, and trade. It celebrates festivals such as Ganesh Chaturthi with great enthusiasm and is known for its vibrant culture. The combination of modern development, historical landmarks, and diverse communities makes Mumbai a unique and energetic city.

2.Jaipur-the pink city

Jaipur has many famous attractions, including Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, City Palace, and Jantar Mantar.
